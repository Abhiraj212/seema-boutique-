Our story

Stitched together over three generations

SR Fashion began as a single cutting table in 1998 and grew into a workshop trusted for suits that fit right the first time.

Workshop, Est. 2012

How it started

A tailor's table, a spool of thread, and a promise

Our founder, Ramesh Sharma, learned pattern-cutting from his father in a one-room shop before opening SR Fashion's first storefront in 2012. The rule he set then still holds: nothing leaves the workshop until it fits properly.

Today the same principle guides every commission — a private consultation, a hand-drafted pattern, and multiple fittings before a single stitch is finished for good.

What guides us

Our values

Fit first

Every pattern is drafted from your own measurements — never adjusted from a standard block.

Honest materials

We source cloth directly from mills we've worked with for years and disclose fibre content plainly.

Time, not shortcuts

Hand-padded lapels and hand-set sleeves take longer — we build that time into every timeline we quote.
