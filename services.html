How a commission runs

From first measurement to final press

01

Consultation

We talk through occasion, budget and fabric, then take a full set of measurements.

02

Pattern & Cut

Your personal block pattern is drafted and the cloth is hand-cut against it.

03

Baste Fitting

A loosely stitched shell is fitted on you first, so the shape can still be adjusted freely.

04

Finish & Press

Buttonholes, lining and final press — then a last fitting before it goes home with you.
